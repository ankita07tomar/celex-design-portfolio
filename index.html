Overview

CELEX Ensemble is a London-based, invitation-only community for luxury wedding professionals. Its flagship experience is the CELEX Sojourn, a familiarisation trip that brings top wedding planners into a destination. This portfolio covers the design and copy I produced across social media, email, event print, web and guest guidelines, including three Sojourns: Vietnam (The Jade Passage, September 2026), Mexico (À La Mexicana, October 2026) and Morocco (December 2026).

Website: celex-ensemble.com
Tools: Canva, WordPress Elementor, Mailchimp, Instagram, LinkedIn, Edits, Claude Design & Code

03Event Printables & Designs

These were internal materials made for Sojourn attendees and partners, so they're shown here as images only rather than shared in full.

Vietnam — The Jade Passage · 4–9 September 2026

Welcome Booklet

Design and copy for the attendee welcome booklet. It gives guests the full event schedule and introduces who is joining them on the trip: their fellow planners and the partners hosting them.

Welcome Suite: cover card + founder's welcome letter

The opening pieces of the guest suite. A lace-frame cover with a lotus motif, and a welcome letter in cream with dragon fretwork corners, setting the trip's palette and voice.

Day-wise invitation cards (16)

One card per experience across six days, from the welcome dinner at the Metropole to dinner inside Dragon Pearl Cave, including the Day 2 Wedding Fest and Calla Bridal Show invitations at Fairmont Hanoi. Each card has a titled theme, short narrative copy, dress code and timing. The look changes per venue (reds for Hanoi, blues for Ha Long Bay, celadon for Hilton Quang Hanh) while the logo, type and card structure stay the same throughout.

Dinner table cards (about 30)

Table cards for the welcome dinner and the other dinner experiences across the Vietnam Sojourn, designed to carry each evening's look onto the table.

Hand-fan series: personalised welcome fans + panel session fans (12)

One fan format used two ways. Nine personalised welcome fans, each with a guest's name in script, made as keepsakes rather than place cards. Three panel fans (Scale, Originality, Decision Making) given to each panelist the day before their session, showing their slot, topic and the question the panel would discuss.

Mexico — À La Mexicana · 10–16 October 2026

Welcome Booklet

Design and copy for the attendee booklet, following the same format as The Jade Passage: the event schedule and an introduction to the planners and partners joining the trip, carried into À La Mexicana's own visual identity.

Panel event flyers (3)

One flyer per panel session, made for partners to share with their own communities to draw attendance. Co-branded with the host venue and partners. The example is the 15 October conference, networking and dinner at Kempinski Hotel Cancún.

Panel Session Invitations

One card per session for attendees, giving the panel title, theme, four focus questions and the panelists. Co-branded with Casa Malca Tulum.

Panel Session Screen Slides

On-screen title slides for each session, matching the invitations. Session title, theme, and panelist photos with name, company and city.
